About the model

Gradient-boosted trees (scikit-learn) predict log annual pay from five inputs. Two extra models trained with quantile loss give the 25th and 75th percentiles. The exported trees reproduce scikit-learn's predictions exactly.

Limits: self-reported 2022 pay converted to USD by Stack Overflow; respondents are not a random sample of developers; company, city, and skills are not modeled. Countries with fewer than 200 responses are grouped as Other.

Data: Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2022, licensed under the Open Database License. Code: github.com/snehalbhatnagar/SalaryPrediction.
